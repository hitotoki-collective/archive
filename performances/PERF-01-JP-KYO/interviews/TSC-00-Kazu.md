I am a shinobue player named Kazuya Sato. About eight years ago, I happened to bump into Kevin, and we talked about doing something together. About two months ago, we performed together for the first time, and we felt a strong connection, so Taro approached me and said, ‘There's this project I'm working on...’ It sounded interesting, so I decided to join in. I'm not really familiar with jazz music, so I didn't know what kind of sounds to use, or anything like that.

But I'm sure the musicians Taro-kun will bring will be amazing, so I decided to just go for it and play with their support. But I didn't want to get too caught up in the details; I just wanted to be natural. The first thing I noticed was the atmosphere of Ozawa Pond. There was almost no wind, and the south wind was blowing gently. From that scenery, there was a tree called ‘Hikari no Ki’ (Tree of Light). I felt an energy filling the air, as if the sky was clearing up.

I tried to take that feeling into myself and began to play. Then, gradually, the guitar came in, followed by the piano. The guitar was like the flickering of light and the piano made me feel the element of water and I drew while imagining the wind and as everyone's sounds and the surrounding nature blended together I enjoyed playing while looking forward to seeing what kind of image Taro-kun would create I played with joy, looking forward to it.

Yes, while playing, I naturally felt the vibrations of the piano and guitar, and I felt as if the wind was weaving.
